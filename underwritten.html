My comps

My comps, as a table you can edit and paste into the UW sheet

Tick comps on the map or in these lists (one selection), then press ✓ Confirm: they move into My comps. There you can edit any cell, mark each comp Low, Mid or High (light green, green, dark green), and copy the rows in the UW sheet’s comp columns. The other tabs list what you ticked, everything shown on the map, and a target’s matches. Hover a row to find it on the map.
